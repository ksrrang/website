Android Vocal Remover

Remove vocals from music and create backing tracks

KSRRangAudio is an Android app that can reduce or remove vocal components from audio files and audio tracks contained in videos to create accompaniment-focused audio. Save the processed result as a separate file and share it with another app or device.

KSRRangAudio vocal removal features

Audio and video support

Process music stored on your device as well as audio tracks contained in video files.

Create accompaniment-focused audio

Reduce or remove vocal components to create results you can use for vocal and instrument practice.

Save and share

Keep vocal-removal results in app-internal storage and share them through messaging, email, or other apps.

How to remove vocals

  1. Choose an audio or video file from local storage or an SMB, FTP, or SFTP server.
  2. Run the vocal removal feature to process the audio.
  3. Listen to the result and save it as a new audio file in app-internal storage.
  4. Share the saved backing track with another app or device when needed.

Vocal removal results depend on the source recording, its mix, and its audio quality. Some vocals or sounds from other instruments may remain in the processed audio.

Practice and edit audio in one app

Play the generated backing track, adjust its speed and pitch, or shape the sound with a 10-band equalizer. Overdub recording is also available for vocal practice, instrument practice, cover recordings, and other audio projects.
