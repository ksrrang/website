Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 23, 2026 · KSRRang explains below how KSRAudio handles information.

Summary

  • Media processing and AI vocal removal are performed for the features requested by the user.
  • Microphone access is used only when recording an overdub.
  • Remote-server connection details are used to connect to SMB, FTP, or SFTP servers selected by the user.
  • Created files are stored in the app's internal storage and leave the app only when the user shares or exports them.

1. Information processed and purposes

Depending on the features you use, KSRAudio may process selected audio or video files, file metadata, microphone audio, editing settings, locally created output files, and remote-server connection details. This information is used only to play or edit media, remove vocals, record overdubs, connect to a server, save results, and perform user-requested sharing.

2. Media files and microphone

The app accesses only media chosen by the user or made available through Android permissions. An original file is read to perform the requested operation. Microphone audio is processed when the user starts recording and is combined with a backing track when requested.

Back up important originals before editing. Do not record another person without the consent required by applicable law.

3. SMB, FTP, and SFTP

The app may process a server address, port, username, password or private key, remote path, and filename to establish a connection requested by the user. These credentials are not customer-support information and should never be sent by email. Traffic passes between the device and the server chosen by the user; server operators and networks may process connection logs under their own policies.

FTP does not encrypt traffic by default. Use SFTP or another appropriately secured connection when handling sensitive data.

4. Storage and sharing

Edited audio, vocal-removal results, and overdub recordings are stored in app-internal storage. They may be deleted if the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. When the user invokes Android's share or export function, the selected recipient app or service processes the file under its own privacy policy.

5. Permissions

  • Media/files: open user-selected media and save or export results.
  • Microphone: record overdubs.
  • Network: connect to user-configured remote servers and provide network-dependent features.
  • Notifications: show playback or processing status where supported.

Permissions can be managed in Android Settings. Disabling one may prevent the related feature from working.

6. Retention, deletion, and security

App-created data remains on the device until the user deletes it, clears app data, or uninstalls the app, subject to Android behavior. Remote credentials remain only as needed for configured connections and can be removed by deleting the connection. We apply reasonable safeguards, but no device, network, or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children, changes, and contact

KSRAudio is not designed to intentionally collect children's personal information. This policy may be updated when features or legal requirements change; the revised effective date will be posted here.
