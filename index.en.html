Android Audio Player & Editor

Play, edit, remove vocals, and overdub

KSRAudio works with audio tracks and audio from video files. Change speed and pitch, shape sound with a 10-band equalizer, remove vocals, record overdubs, and save the result—all in one Android app. Open media directly from local storage or SMB, FTP, and SFTP servers, then share your finished files with other apps and devices.

Everything you need to work with audio

Audio and video playback

Open and process audio files as well as audio tracks contained in video files.

Speed and pitch

Adjust playback speed and pitch independently for practice, listening, and production.

10-band equalizer

Fine-tune ten frequency bands to create the sound you want.

Vocal removal

Reduce or remove vocal components and save an accompaniment-focused version.

Overdub recording

Record vocals or instruments while listening to a backing track and combine both tracks.

Remote media

Play and edit audio or video from SMB, FTP, and SFTP servers.

Save edited results

Save audio with your speed, pitch, and EQ settings as a new file in app storage.

Share files

Send edits, vocal-removal results, and overdubs to email, messaging, cloud, or other apps.

Practical player controls

Use playback, pause, seek, repeat, and other everyday audio controls.

A simple workflow

1. Choose a file

Open audio or video from local storage or a remote server.

2. Shape the sound

Set speed, pitch, and the 10-band EQ.

3. Process or record

Remove vocals or record an overdub when needed.

4. Save and share

Store the result in the app and share it with another app or device.
